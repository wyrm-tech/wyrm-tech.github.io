Start with a workshop. Build from there.

Workshop→Advisory→Hands-on

  1. Workshop Clarity

    Lead with a focused workshop for every new client. It surfaces their most useful AI opportunities, introduces practical skills, and gives everyone a shared next step.

  2. Advisory Partnership

    After the workshop, make Advisory the natural next conversation for clients who want to learn and apply AI themselves. Monthly or quarterly guidance helps them build confidence, review priorities, and keep the work moving.

  3. Hands-on Action

    Offer Hands-on when a client wants WyrmTech to achieve a defined outcome for them. We set up the solution, test it on real work, and help the team put it to use.

The preferred path is Workshop, then Advisory, then Hands-on work as a client is ready. A client who already knows the outcome they need can move from their workshop directly into a Hands-on project.

Your consultancy allocation

25% of contract earnings is allocated to Consultancy for the client delivery you take on. This role can be combined with Sales or Tool Builder participation.

Build the library behind the work

Contribute to the AI libraries WyrmTech is developing for use during consultancy. Help create, improve, test, and document reusable tools and workflows that consultants can apply with clients.

Contributions could support work like financial reporting, cloud deployment, or turning a product vision into development requirements.

Your tool-building allocation

25% of contract earnings is reserved for the Tool Builder pool. Your portion of that pool will be based on your contribution to the library and how much of the library is used on a per engagement basis.

The contribution method is still being defined. Selecting Tool Builder represents participation in the pool; it does not assign the full 25% to one person.
