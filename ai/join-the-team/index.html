Your sales allocation

Earn 25% of contract earnings on deals you close. For longer-term engagements, this applies for up to the first 12 months of the established engagement.

After the first 12 months, earn 5% of collected contract revenue for any renewal engagement you actively secure, paid for up to the first 12 months of the renewed term.

Be the people behind the concierge service

Build a working relationship with each client: understand their goals, explain the options, and help them make progress. Through The Steward, coach individuals as they apply AI themselves. Through The Guild, facilitate group consultations and training that help teams work toward shared priorities. Through The Butler, take responsibility for building and testing the agreed solution and training their team. Draw on our shared AI libraries as you deliver.

Your consultancy allocation

25% of contract earnings is allocated to Consultancy for the client delivery you take on. This role can be combined with Sales or Tool Builder participation.

Build the tools behind AI Concierge

Contribute to the AI libraries WyrmTech is developing for The Steward, The Guild, and The Butler. Create, improve, test, and document reusable tools and workflows that our team can use in individual guidance, team sessions, and project delivery.

Contributions could support work like financial reporting, cloud deployment, or turning a product vision into development requirements.

Your tool-building allocation

25% of contract earnings is reserved for the Tool Builder pool. Your portion of that pool will be based on your contribution to the library and how much of the library is used on a per engagement basis.

The contribution method is still being defined. Selecting Tool Builder represents participation in the pool; it does not assign the full 25% to one person.

01 / We guide

The Steward

Personal guidance for clients who lead the work. Each plan sets a consultation cadence; Select and Signature add training.

Essential

For self-directed clients who want expert perspective at key decision points.

What the client gets

  • An annual in-depth consultation tailored to their needs.
  • A chance to review priorities and choose practical next steps.
Client benefit

Clear direction while they move forward at their own pace.

Select

For clients who want a regular rhythm of learning and feedback.

What the client gets

  • Quarterly in-depth consultations tailored to their needs.
  • Shared training on common AI concepts.
Client benefit

Practical skills and guidance as they apply AI to their work.

Signature

For clients actively working through specific AI opportunities.

What the client gets

  • Monthly in-depth consultations tailored to their needs.
  • Focused training on specific AI concepts.
Client benefit

Frequent guidance and deeper skills tied to their priorities.

Before quoting: Match the consultation and training level to the client’s goals, then view the current Steward plans for pricing and billing options. If they want our team to build a solution, discuss a Butler project.

03 / We deliver

The Butler

The delivery side of AI Concierge. Clients approve a fixed bid for an agreed project, then WyrmTech builds and tests the solution and trains their team to use it.

Per engagement pricing

Minimum

$10,000 per engagement

One agreed price for a defined scope of work. The final quote depends on the project.

Scope & schedule Agree on deliverables and a delivery schedule before work begins.

Client benefit

A working solution for an agreed business task, with the build handled by WyrmTech and training to put it into use.

What’s included

  • A kickoff conversation and guidance at agreed project checkpoints.
  • Build-out and testing of the agreed solution by the WyrmTech team.
  • Training to help the client’s team use the solution in their work.
  • A point of contact for questions while the project is in progress.
  • Community access to connect and learn alongside others.

Before quoting: Agree on the outcome, scope, deliverables, and delivery schedule with the delivery team. Quote one fixed price for the engagement, with a $10,000 minimum.

WyrmTech retains 25% to support the business behind each engagement. This covers payment processing, AI tools and licensing, and program administration. Funds remaining after expenses support financial reserves, reinvestment in the program, and company profit.
