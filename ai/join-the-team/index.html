The Steward→The Butler

  1. The Steward We guide

    Introduce AI Concierge as a team that understands the client’s business and helps them use AI. Recommend The Steward when they want to learn and lead the work, with monthly or quarterly guidance to build confidence and work through priorities.

  2. The Butler We deliver

    Offer The Butler when a client wants to put a defined project in WyrmTech’s hands. We agree on the outcome, build and test the solution, and train their team to use it.

Clients can begin with either service. The Steward can lead into a project through The Butler when a client identifies work they want us to deliver. Match the service to their goals and how involved they want to be.

Your sales allocation

Earn 25% of contract earnings on deals you close. For longer-term engagements, this applies for up to the first 12 months of the established engagement.

After the first 12 months, earn 5% of collected contract revenue for any renewal engagement you actively secure, paid for up to the first 12 months of the renewed term.

Be the people behind the concierge service

Build a working relationship with each client: understand their goals, explain the options, and help them make progress. Through The Steward, coach them as they apply AI themselves. Through The Butler, take responsibility for building and testing the agreed solution and training their team. Draw on our shared AI libraries as you deliver.

The Steward · Monthly

A monthly advisory session and support between meetings to help clients make decisions, build confidence, and apply AI in their work.

The Steward · Quarterly

A focused engagement each quarter to review progress, reset priorities, and work through the next opportunity. A fit for clients who prefer a less frequent cadence.

The Butler · Project delivery

Deliver a solution for a defined business task, from agreed scope through build, testing, and training. Keep the client involved in decisions and informed about progress.

Agree on responsibilities, scope, and support with each client before the engagement begins.

Your consultancy allocation

25% of contract earnings is allocated to Consultancy for the client delivery you take on. This role can be combined with Sales or Tool Builder participation.

Build the tools behind AI Concierge

Contribute to the AI libraries WyrmTech is developing for The Steward and The Butler. Create, improve, test, and document reusable tools and workflows that our team can use to guide clients and deliver their solutions.

Contributions could support work like financial reporting, cloud deployment, or turning a product vision into development requirements.

Your tool-building allocation

25% of contract earnings is reserved for the Tool Builder pool. Your portion of that pool will be based on your contribution to the library and how much of the library is used on a per engagement basis.

The contribution method is still being defined. Selecting Tool Builder represents participation in the pool; it does not assign the full 25% to one person.
