Start with a workshop. Build from there.

Workshop→Advisory→Hands-on

  1. Workshop Clarity

    Lead with a focused workshop for every new client. It surfaces their most useful AI opportunities, introduces practical skills, and gives everyone a shared next step.

  2. Advisory Partnership

    After the workshop, make Advisory the natural next conversation for clients who want to learn and apply AI themselves. Monthly or quarterly guidance helps them build confidence, review priorities, and keep the work moving.

  3. Hands-on Action

    Offer Hands-on when a client wants WyrmTech to achieve a defined outcome for them. We set up the solution, test it on real work, and help the team put it to use.

The preferred path is Workshop, then Advisory, then Hands-on work as a client is ready. A client who already knows the outcome they need can move from their workshop directly into a Hands-on project.

Your sales allocation

Earn 25% of contract earnings on deals you close. For longer-term engagements, this applies for up to the first 12 months of the established engagement.

After the first 12 months, earn 5% of collected contract revenue for any renewal engagement you actively secure, paid for up to the first 12 months of the renewed term.

Be the person who helps it happen

Work directly with clients to identify opportunities, teach practical AI skills, and do the hands-on work that puts a solution into use. Draw on our shared AI libraries as you deliver.

Monthly engagement

Regular working sessions, active support, and hands-on progress as the client builds confidence and capability.

Quarterly engagement

A focused engagement each quarter to review progress, reset priorities, and work through the next opportunity. A fit for clients who prefer a less frequent cadence.

Set the scope and level of hands-on support with each client, for either cadence.

Your consultancy allocation

25% of contract earnings is allocated to Consultancy for the client delivery you take on. This role can be combined with Sales or Tool Builder participation.

Build the library behind the work

Contribute to the AI libraries WyrmTech is developing for use during consultancy. Help create, improve, test, and document reusable tools and workflows that consultants can apply with clients.

Contributions could support work like financial reporting, cloud deployment, or turning a product vision into development requirements.

Your tool-building allocation

25% of contract earnings is reserved for the Tool Builder pool. Your portion of that pool will be based on your contribution to the library and how much of the library is used on a per engagement basis.

The contribution method is still being defined. Selecting Tool Builder represents participation in the pool; it does not assign the full 25% to one person.

01 Workshop 02 Advisory 03 Hands-on

01 / Clarity

Workshop

Give new clients a practical first experience with AI. Choose the level that matches their confidence and experience.

Beginner

$200 one time

For clients who are new to AI and want to know where to start.

Workshop focus

  • Understand what AI can help with in everyday business work.
  • Practice clear prompts for tasks such as drafting and summarizing.
  • Learn to check answers and think carefully about what information to share.
Client benefit

The confidence to try a useful first task.

Term One-time workshop.

Intermediate

$250 one time

For clients who have tried AI and want more consistent, useful results.

Workshop focus

  • Improve prompts with better context, examples, and clear instructions.
  • Turn a recurring business task into a repeatable AI workflow.
  • Review the results and identify where human judgment belongs.
Client benefit

A more repeatable approach to applying AI at work.

Term One-time workshop.

Advanced

$300 one time

For confident AI users who want to explore more involved business workflows.

Workshop focus

  • Break a larger business process into steps AI can support.
  • Explore opportunities to connect tools and reuse existing workflows.
  • Identify review points and the next opportunity to develop.
Client benefit

A clearer path from individual tasks to broader business use.

Term One-time workshop.

The next conversation: After the workshop, review the opportunities together. Recommend Advisory for clients who want to keep learning and doing, or Hands-on for a defined outcome they want us to deliver.

The client leads the work, with an expert to help them make decisions, work through questions, and keep learning.

Quarterly

For self-directed clients who want a focused check-in each quarter.

  • Community access to connect and learn alongside others.
Client benefit

Expert guidance at key decision points while they move at their own pace.

Term Quarterly engagement.

Monthly

$1,000 per month

For clients actively putting AI into practice who want regular guidance.

What’s included

  • One 1-hour advisory session per month to work through questions and next steps.
  • Expert access for questions between sessions, without scheduling a meeting.
  • Community access to connect and learn alongside others.
Client benefit

03 / Action

Hands-on

For clients who want an outcome delivered. WyrmTech takes on the build and trains their team to use the solution.

Project

Starting at

$1,000 per month

A minimum of $1,000 per month, with pricing based on the scope of the project.

Term Agreed project term. Scope and duration determine the total cost.

Client benefit

A working solution for an agreed business task, with training to put it into use.

What’s included

  • Advisory support starting with a 30-minute session each month.
  • Build-out and testing of the agreed solution by the WyrmTech team.
  • Training to help the client’s team use the solution in their work.
  • Expert access for questions between sessions.
  • Community access to connect and learn alongside others.

Before quoting: Agree on the outcome, deliverables, and project term with the delivery team. The monthly minimum is a starting point; the final price depends on the work required.
