Lead follow-up · Home services

Give every new lead a next step.

For the contractor whose slow replies let ready-to-buy leads choose someone else.
Illustrative added revenue $3,000 / month before project costs Year-one ROI: —

The everyday problem

New inquiries arrive during jobs and after hours. The owner catches up at night, asks the same questions, and sometimes loses track of who still needs a reply.

A workflow with AI

AI sorts each inquiry, drafts a relevant reply asking for missing details, and flags promising jobs for prompt follow-up. The owner checks the reply, confirms availability, and sends it.

What could improve

More inquiries can become paid jobs. Prompt, relevant follow-up gives interested prospects a clear path from first message to completed work.

Start smallTry one website inquiry form and one service area.

Measure the changeInquiry-to-completed-job rate, average job revenue, and time to first reply.
