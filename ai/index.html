01 Lead follow-up · Home services Give every new lead a next step. For the contractor whose inbox fills up while the team is on the job. Projected ROI:: $300 / month

The everyday problem

New inquiries arrive during jobs and after hours. The owner catches up at night, asks the same questions, and sometimes loses track of who still needs a reply.

A workflow with AI

AI reads each inquiry, summarizes the job, and drafts a reply asking for missing details. The workflow creates a follow-up task; the owner checks the reply and confirms availability before sending.

What could improve

More opportunities to book work. A clear next step for each lead, less inbox sorting, and fewer inquiries forgotten during a busy day.

Start smallTry one website inquiry form and one service area.

Measure the changeTime to first reply, unanswered inquiries, and bookings.

02 Proposals & estimates · Professional services Turn a good sales call into a proposal. For the small agency that spends its evenings rewriting the same documents. Projected ROI:: $720 / month

The everyday problem

A promising call ends with scattered notes. Someone copies an old proposal, rewrites the scope, and checks for details left over from the previous client.

A workflow with AI

AI turns the call notes, approved service descriptions, and a proposal template into a first draft. It flags missing information. The owner confirms the scope, prices, and delivery dates before sending.

What could improve

Less writing between the conversation and the sale. The team starts with a relevant draft and spends its time refining the offer.

03 Customer support · Retail & online shops Answer the repeat questions. Focus on the exceptions. For the shop where the same product and returns questions interrupt the day. Projected ROI:: $400 / month

The everyday problem

Staff repeatedly look up product details, care instructions, and return policies. Routine emails compete with customers who need individual attention.

A workflow with AI

AI finds relevant answers in approved product information and policies, then drafts a reply with a link to its source. Staff review it; uncertain questions, complaints, and refund decisions go to a person.

What could improve

Less time searching for the same answers. More consistent replies and more room for the conversations that need a personal touch.

05 Reporting & operations · Growing teams Start the week with a clear picture of the business. For the owner piecing together sales, bookings, and unpaid invoices by hand. Projected ROI:: $360 / month

The everyday problem

The numbers live in different systems. Preparing the weekly update takes so long that slow bookings, overdue payments, or unfinished work get noticed late.

A workflow with AI

AI turns approved weekly reports into a short briefing using their existing totals. It highlights changes, links back to the source reports, and drafts a follow-up list. The owner verifies the figures and assigns next steps.

What could improve

Less report assembly. More time to act. A repeatable weekly review makes it easier to spot what needs attention and give each follow-up an owner.

Start smallUse two existing reports and three questions you ask every week.

Measure the changeReport preparation time, accuracy, and follow-ups completed.
